Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Smart Appointment Receptionist is currently a development and testing project for connecting an appointment-management service with Google Calendar.

Information we access

When you authorize the application with Google, the application may access the minimum Google Calendar data needed to provide its features, including your calendar list, free/busy availability, and calendar events.

How information is used

Google Calendar data is used only to let the application display available calendars, check appointment availability, and create, update, or manage appointment events that you request.

Data sharing

We do not sell Google user data. Google Calendar data is not shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the service or where required by law.

Data retention

During development and testing, access credentials and calendar-related data may be stored only as needed to maintain the Google Calendar connection and provide the requested functionality. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials and data handled by the application. This project is still in development and should not yet be considered a production service.

Your choices

You can revoke the application's access to your Google Account at any time from your Google Account security and permissions settings.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or the application's use of Google data, contact the developer through the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
